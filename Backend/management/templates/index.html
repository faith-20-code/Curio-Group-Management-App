About Curio

This web application is designed to help university students manage group projects more efficiently. It allows group leaders to create tasks, assign work, and track progress, while members can update their task status and communicate easily. The system also includes AI-assisted task allocation to help distribute work fairly among group members. Our goal is to simplify collaboration and improve productivity during academic projects.
